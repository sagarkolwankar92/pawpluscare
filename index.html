Frequently asked questions

What makes Paw+ supplements unique?
Our blends use science-backed super ingredients like glucosamine for joint comfort, probiotics for gut balance, and omega-3s for coat health.
How should I serve Paw+ supplements?
Use Paw+ as a daily topper mixed into your pet's regular food. For best results, add a little water before stirring it in - this helps the powder blend smoothly and improves the taste.
What is the correct dosage?
Dosage instructions are clearly listed on your product packaging and on each product's page. Just follow the directions provided there.
How long until I see results?
Most pet parents report noticeable improvements within 2-6 weeks of consistent use. Factors include your pet's age, diet, and specific health condition.
Why can't I just add these nutrients via dog food?
Commercial pet foods often contain beneficial ingredients - but usually at levels too low to have a therapeutic impact. Our supplements fill those nutritional gaps safely and effectively.
Are Paw+ supplements safe?
Yes - when used as directed. Our products are formulated with pet-safe dosages and science-backed research. Always follow instructions and monitor your pet; discontinue use if any adverse reaction occurs.
Can I mix two Paw+ variants?
Yes - introduce them gradually. Start with half the recommended dose of each and alternate them through the day (one in the morning, one in the evening). After a few days with no adverse effects, move to full doses. A slow, deliberate introduction helps prevent digestive upset.
